## Executive Summary

SPECTRA-IDS is a comprehensive research platform that introduces a breakthrough quantum-resilient autonomous threat intelligence framework specifically designed for 6G-enabled IoT edge networks. The platform addresses critical challenges in detecting AI-driven botnets, adversarial machine learning attacks, and zero-day threats through a novel combination of:

- **Chameleon Drift Layer (CDL)** - Behavioral fingerprinting and drift detection
- **Seismic Pre-shock Detector (SPD)** - Predictive threat anticipation
- **Predator Pursuit Engine (PPE)** - Adaptive resource allocation
- **Hybrid CNN-BiLSTM-Attention Architecture** - Multi-model ensemble detection
- **Federated Learning Integration** - Distributed threat intelligence
- **Post-Quantum Security Layer** - Quantum-resistant communications
- **Energy Intelligence System** - Detection cost optimization
- **Explainable AI Framework** - Interpretable decision-making
